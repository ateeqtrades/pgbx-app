PGBX App: What We Need from PGBX

Prepared by DXL Solutions · 16 November 2026

Where we are: the PGBX customer app (Android, iPhone and web), the admin panel for PGBX staff and the dealer app are built and tested. The app is in closed testing on Google Play. To go live with real customers, we need the items below from PGBX.

Needed first blocks launch   Needed soon needed before the real money pilot   Before launch needed before the public release

1. Legal and approvals

#ItemDetailsPriority
1Authorisation letterA signed letter on PGBX letterhead allowing DXL Solutions to publish and manage the PGBX app on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store on PGBX's behalf.Needed first
2Licence or registration documentsPGBX's legal entity name and registration number, and the licence or registration that allows PGBX to deal in precious metals. Both stores ask for this for finance apps.Needed first
3Shariah approvalWritten approval from PGBX's Shariah adviser or board, with the adviser's name and date. Until then the app does not show a "Shariah compliant" badge.Before launch
4Legal review of terms and privacy policyPGBX's lawyer to review the draft Terms of Use and Privacy Policy (already written) and confirm the items marked for PGBX.Before launch
5Contact detailsOfficial support phone and WhatsApp, support email, privacy contact, and the website or domain the app should use (for example app.pgbx.com.pk).Needed soon

2. Service providers (PGBX chooses and signs up)

#ItemDetailsPriority
6Payment gatewayWhich gateway customers will pay through (cards, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa or Raast). PGBX opens the merchant account; we connect it (about 2 to 4 days).Needed first
7Identity check (CNIC) providerA provider for CNIC and selfie checks, required before a customer's first purchase. We connect it (about 3 to 5 days).Needed first
8SMS providerFor login codes sent to customers' phones. A local Pakistani SMS provider is cheapest; Twilio also works.Needed soon
9Hosting and database accountsVercel (hosting) and Supabase (database) accounts in PGBX's name, or approval for DXL to run them and bill PGBX.Needed soon

3. Business details for the app

#ItemDetailsPriority
10Products and pricingBar sizes sold, PGBX's premium on each product, and the buy/sell spread.Needed soon
11Limits and feesPer order and daily purchase limits, collection fee, storage fee or time limit, gift and appraisal charges.Needed soon
12Payout and refund timesHow quickly PGBX pays customers who sell back, and the bank account PGBX pays from.Needed soon
13DealersList of dealers: name, address, map location, opening hours, contact person.Needed soon
14Opening stockStock of each bar at each dealer and in the vault, with serial numbers.Needed soon
15Record keepingHow long customer records must be kept by law after an account closes.Before launch

4. People and operations

#ItemDetailsPriority
16Staff for the admin panelNames and emails for: administrator, operations (orders, refunds, payouts), support (confirms rates in chat). Each needs an authenticator app on their phone.Needed soon
17Dealer counter staffOne login per dealer counter, plus a short training session on handing over bars.Needed soon
18Support hoursHours when PGBX support will answer rate chats. Every purchase waits for a rate confirmation, including during the store review.Needed soon
19TestersPGBX staff with Android phones (Gmail address each) to join the closed test and try the app.Needed first
20Real money pilotApproval for a few small real purchases, one collection and one sell back before launch.Before launch
